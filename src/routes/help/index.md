# Getting Started

## Rules

[Click here](/rules) to view our rules. Violating our rules may result in a ban. Appeal a ban on our [forms page](/forms).

## Ranks

You obtain ranks on Acorn by voting. For a list of all ranks and their capabilities, please visit [the ranks page](/help/ranks). You can check your progress to a certain rank by typing `/votetotal`. You obtain a new rank at 10, 25, 50, 100, 250, 500, 1000, and 2000 votes.

`/vote` will provide you with 4 links. Click on them, provide your username, and submit your vote. Voting will give you 1 crate key, 1 vote point, and it will increase your vote total by 1. For more information on voting, [click here](/vote) to visit the voting page.

### Crate Keys

Crate keys can be redeemed at `/warp crates` for money, claim blocks, and rare items. Use `/keys` to check your keys.

### Vote Points

You can spend vote points in the `/voteshop` to buy special perks like `/fly` and infinite homes.

### Homes

Ranking up will increase the amount of `/homes` you can create. Create a home with `/sethome [home name]`. List your homes with `/homes`. Delete a home with `/delhome [home name]`. Visit any of your homes with `/home [home name]`.

Zombies can only set one home, and can omit the `[home name]` variable in the above commands.

### Staff

You can apply for staff on our [forms page](/forms). You must be at least stray rank to apply for staff.

## Preventing Grief

You can use a golden shovel to make claims. You'll obtain a free golden shovel by voting for the fourth time. Other players don't have permission to build, open containers, or even push buttons and open doors in your claims. In order to give your friends permission to build, use `/trust` while standing in your claim. In order to give your friends permission to open containers such as chests and furnaces, use `/containertrust`. In order to give your friends permission to use buttons, levers, and doors, use `/accesstrust`. If you would like to give everyone permission in your claim, you can use `public` as the playername. You can obtain claim blocks using `/buyclaimblocks` or by using crate keys at `/warp crates`.

You can also make single-block locks with `/lock`. These blocks can be unlocked with `/unlock`. You can add players to the lock with `/cmodify`. If you want to create a donation chest where players can add items in but can't take items out, use `/cdonation` on an unlocked chest. If you want to create a container where players can view the contents but can't add or remove items from it, you can use `/cdisplay` on an unlocked chest.

If you choose to use both of these protection methods: please note that to add someone to a container, they must be added to both the claim and the lock. For a donation chest, players must be given at least containertrust permissions in the claim as well.

For a more in-depth explanation on claims, please [click here](/help/preventing_grief).

## Support

If you need attention from a staff member, you can either create a `/ticket` or post a message in our Discord. When both options are available, please only complete one of them. Tickets are generally resolved faster through Discord messages, and we would recommend creating only a ticket there when possible.

### Tickets

You can create a ticket with `/ticket [issue]`. For instance, you would type `/ticket My beacon was griefed. Some iron blocks are missing` if your beacon was griefed and you had forgotten to claim it. Please be aware that tickets may take up to 24 hours to resolve but can be resolved in just a few minutes depending on the time that they're created. Please create the ticket at the location of the issue.

### Discord

Alternatively, you can receive support by requesting support in our #support channel. Please describe the issue in detail and provide the coordinates if applicable. You can join our Discord [here](https://discord.gg/CRBQKYA).

### Emergencies

If there is an emergency, such as a raid of spammers, please type `@staff` in-game and describe the issue. For example, you could type `@staff the server is being raided by spammers.` Typing `@staff` will send a mobile or desktop notification to all staff members, so please refrain from using it when not absolutely necessary.

## Community

Our server is heavily centered around our community. Please join our Discord [here](https://discord.gg/CRBQKYA) to talk while offline, share memes, and play games.

All plugins are visible in `/pl`, and you can check the TPS through `/tps`. ## Economy

Acorn has several methods of making money.

### Voting

Voting gives crate keys. You can redeem your crate keys at `/warp crates` for money.

### Server Shop

You can sell select items to the server shop at `/warp shop`. The server shop only has a few items to encourage players to make their own chestshops as part of our goal for a 99% player-run economy.

### Player Shops

Players make shops with chest shops. Some shops allow you to sell to them. You can also make your own chest shop. [Click here](/shops) for a tutorial on creating your own chest shop. You can ban people from using your shops using the command `/shopban [username]`.

### Jobs

After talking with another player and agreeing on a task and payment, complete the job and receive the payment. Players can pay others with the `/pay` command.

### Talking

You gain $2 per message. Please keep in mind that money from talking has a cooldown, so spamming won't earn you money! You'll earn money for both in-game messages and messages in Discord. You'll need to link your Minecraft account before you can earn money from Discord messages.

## Services

### Heads

Decorative heads cost $100 each. You can use `/hdb` to find heads and clicking one will purchase it. You can also obtain player heads for $250 each using the command `/phead [username]`.

### PvP Arenas

PvP arenas cost a one-time fee and can be of any reasonable size. This fee depends on the complexity of the arena's shape. PvP can also be turned toggled using `/pvp on` and `/pvp off`.

### Warps

Warps cost $50/day. Use `/iwarp create [name] [days]` to make a warp. You can renew a warp at any time. For more information, please visit our [warps page](/help/warps).
